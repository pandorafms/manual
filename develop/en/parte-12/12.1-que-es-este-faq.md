# What is this FAQ

This FAQ gathers the questions that come up most often about Pandora FMS. They
come from the two knowledge bases the team maintains (the community one and the
official support one) and they are **reviewed one by one** before being
published. It is not a dump: only what is still true goes in here, checked
against the code of the current version.

Three things to get the most out of it:

- **It is ordered by topic**, not by origin or age. The index is right below.
- **Every answer goes to the point**: what to check, in what order and with
  what command. When the topic has its own chapter on the site, it is linked,
  so that a procedure already told is not repeated here.
- **What no longer applies is left out.** The original entries are years old:
  discontinued operating systems, old versions and features that no longer
  exist. If something is missing, it is because it should not be there.

And to get your bearings before searching: if you are going to **install**
Pandora FMS for the first time, start with
[Part 01](../parte-1/1.1-instalacion.md); if you are looking for the **exact
detail** of a token or an option,
[Part 09](../parte-9/9.1-referencia-completa-de-configuracion-del-endpoint.md).
This FAQ is for when something does not work or you do not know where to start.
